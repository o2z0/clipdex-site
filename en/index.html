FEATURES

ClipDex, in detail.

Find what you copied in your history and pick it to copy it again. Back in your app, press ⌘V to paste.

  • Each row shows the icon of the app you copied from
  • Select several items to paste them in one go, newest first, one per line
  • Drag items straight into other apps
  • 30 items free; 50–1,000 with Pro (200 by default)
  • ClipDex asks on first launch whether to keep what you copy. Nothing is saved until you agree

Keep snippets, code, images and videos in groups, ready to use again.

  • Text: 10 items free, 300 with Pro
  • Images and videos are counted separately: 5 free / 300 with Pro
  • Reordering, moving and deleting can be undone step by step with ⌘Z

Search history and Saved Items together, and filter by app, group or date.

  • Works with mixed Japanese, English and code
  • Combine filters such as “Today” and “Slack”

Press ⇧⌘V to open the panel near your cursor. You can change the shortcut.

  • Switch between history, Saved Items and groups with tabs
  • Preview images at a larger size, with dimensions and file size
  • Close with Esc or ⌘W. Hold ⌘ while picking an item to stay in the panel and keep copying

See how much you copied and which apps you copied from, period by period.

  • Breakdown by the app you copied from
  • Copy counts and total characters
  • Day-of-week × hour heatmap
  • Five periods: today, this week, last 4 weeks, this month, this year (the free version covers the last 7 days)

PRIVACY

Your clipboard data never leaves your Mac

ClipDex sends no data anywhere.
No analytics server, no account.
Statistics and search all happen on your Mac.

Nothing is sent out

The app sends nothing over the network. No account needed.

Saving content is your choice

You decide whether the actual content you copy is kept. It is off by default.

Password managers are excluded

Eight apps, including 1Password, Bitwarden and Keychain Access, are excluded by default. You can add more.

Pause any time

Pause for 15 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour from the menu bar. Statistics and clipboard history can be deleted in one step, too.

See the Privacy Policy for details.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the App Store version differ from the Full version?

The biggest difference is whether picking an item pastes it for you.

  • App Store version (this page): picking an item copies it and switches back to the previous app; you press ⌘V yourself. It asks for no permissions at all
  • Full version (distributed on this site): picking an item switches back to the previous app and pastes it. Paste Stack (paste in order) and paste tracking and records are included. It uses the macOS Accessibility permission

Everything else, including history, Saved Items, search and reports, is the same.

Doesn’t it paste automatically after I pick an item?

No. Pasting into other apps automatically requires the macOS Accessibility permission, a very powerful permission that lets an app control keyboard input across the whole system. ClipDex chose to ask for no permissions at all.

Instead, picking a row copies its content and takes you back to the app you were just using. All you press is ⌘V.

If you want automatic pasting, use the Full version distributed on this site.

What is transferred when I drag an item?

Dragging a single image or video passes it as a file. Anything else (text, or a selection of several items) is passed as text.

How much rich text formatting is kept when I copy text?

Bold and colored text is kept with its formatting. Pasting keeps formatting by default; hold ⌥ while picking to get plain text just that once (you can swap the default in Settings).

Copying with ⌘C always keeps formatting, regardless of that setting. Several items passed together, or a saved item whose text you have edited, become plain text.

If the formatting data is too large (2 MB per format in history, 512 KB in Saved Items), only that format is dropped and the text is kept.

How are images saved?

Images, including JPEGs, are converted to PNG*1 when saved. When you pass an image to another app as a file, it keeps its original name with the extension changed (photo.jpg → photo.png).

Saving images is off by default; turn it on in Settings. Images are counted separately from text clips.

*1 PNG is a lossless format. Copied images arrive in many forms, such as JPEG or TIFF, depending on the app, so ClipDex stores them in one format without losing quality. As a result, photos can take more space than the original JPEG.

Can I set any shortcut I like?

Any combination that includes ⌘, ⌃ or ⌥. A key with only ⇧, or with no modifier at all, cannot be used.

How far back can I undo?

Up to 20 steps. While you are typing in a text field, that field’s own undo takes priority.

How many items can I keep?
  • Clipboard history (text): 30 items free / 50–1,000 with Pro (200 by default)
  • Clipboard history (images): 15 images, 50 MB total, 10 MB each free / 50–500 images, 1 GB total, 100 MB each with Pro
  • Saved Items (text): 10 free / 300 with Pro
  • Saved Items (images and videos): 5 free / 300 with Pro; up to 100 MB per image and 500 MB per video

Clipboard history drops the oldest items once it reaches the limit. Saved Items are never removed; you simply can’t add more once you reach the limit. Upgrading to Pro raises the limits, but history items that were already dropped do not come back.

What does a backup contain?

Settings and statistics (the numbers behind the reports). Clipboard history, Saved Items, and image and video files are not included.

What is deleted when I erase my data?

“All Data” in Settings deletes statistics and clipboard history. Saved Items are kept; to remove them, select the items and delete them.
