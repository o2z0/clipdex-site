ClipDex Full Edition (direct)

Sold directly by the developer. It is a separate build from the App Store edition, with different features and a different license model.

What the Full Edition adds

  • Select to paste — pick an item from History or Saved Items and it is pasted into the previous app automatically (the App Store edition copies and returns; you press ⌘V). Paste Stack (paste items in order) is included.
  • Paste tracking — ⌘V counts and time of day, breakdown by destination app, and Paste Records with search (Pro).
  • Everything in the App Store edition — clipboard history, saved items, reports, exports.

The Full Edition uses the macOS Accessibility permission (for auto‑paste and ⌘V tracking). The App Store edition is designed to request no permissions at all.

License model

  • One‑time purchase. You can use it free with the same limits as the App Store free edition; enter a key to unlock Pro.
  • Versions released within one year (365 days) of your purchase are covered. When that period ends, the versions you already have keep working forever. Buy an update license only if you want newer versions.
  • One key works on every Mac you personally use. No device registration.

Buy a license

Payments are processed by Stripe (Link). Cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted. The developer never sees your card details.

After you buy

  1. Your license key is shown on the confirmation page and emailed to you (one line starting with CLIPDEX-).
  2. Download the Full Edition, open Settings → General → ClipDex Pro, paste the key, press Activate.
  3. Didn’t get the key? Use the resend form.
