ClipDex Privacy Policy (Full version)

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This is the privacy policy for ClipDex downloaded from this site (the Full version). For the version distributed on the Mac App Store, see the App Store version privacy policy.

ClipDex ("the app") manages clipboard history and Saved Items (snippets, images and videos) on macOS. By its very nature, the app touches highly sensitive information, so it is designed never to send the data it collects from copying and pasting anywhere.

1. Network communication

The only network communication the app performs is checking for and downloading updates (section 1-1). License keys are verified on your Mac without any network access. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no external crash reporting.

Of the data the app handles (clipboard contents, history, Saved Items, usage statistics, paste records, license key), none is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Buying a license, buying a renewal, and resending a license key are done in your web browser on this site, not in the app. The information handled then is described in section 1-2.

1-1. Update checks

To find out whether a newer version exists, the app contacts clipdex.o2z0.com once every 24 hours (including at launch if 24 hours have passed since the last check) using the Sparkle framework. If a newer version exists, it is downloaded from github.com (the developer's release repository) after you confirm. clipdex.o2z0.com is served by GitHub Pages, so both destinations are services operated by GitHub.

  • What is sent: the app name, app version and Sparkle version (in the HTTP User-Agent). Sparkle's option to send information about your Mac's configuration is turned off. Clipboard contents, history, Saved Items, your license key and any other data are never sent
  • What reaches the destination: as with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address. GitHub handles it under GitHub's privacy policy
  • Separation: the update check and download are performed by a helper (an XPC service) separate from the app. The app itself is built without the entitlement for outgoing connections

You can turn automatic checks off in Settings → General → "Check for updates automatically". With it off, the app only connects when you choose "Check for Updates…" from the menu bar.

1-2. Buying, renewing and resending a license

Licenses are sold using the following services.

  • Payment (Stripe / Link) — payment takes place on a checkout page provided by Stripe. The developer never receives card numbers or other payment details. What you enter on the checkout page (such as your email address) is collected and recorded by Stripe as an order and handled under Stripe's privacy policy. The developer can view orders and buyer information (including the email address used for purchase) in the Stripe dashboard. The license server receives order details from Stripe (order ID, amount, currency, whether a discount was applied, the purchase email address, etc.) to confirm the order and send the license key, but does not store the email address itself (see the table below)
  • License server (Cloudflare) — the only server the developer operates, running on Cloudflare Workers. It is used only when you buy or renew a license, or ask for a license key to be resent
  • Email delivery (Resend) — emails containing your license key are sent through Resend. Resend receives the recipient address and the email body, which includes the license key
  • Abuse prevention (Cloudflare Turnstile) — the resend form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated submissions. For this check, your IP address is passed to Cloudflare

1-3. Crossgrade requests from the App Store version

Only if you own App Store Pro and request the Full version discount, we keep the following. This happens by email; the app is not involved.

  • What we keep: the email you send and the details shown in the attached receipt image (Apple purchase date, item and name)
  • Purpose: confirming the App Store Pro purchase and issuing a discount code
  • Retention: the email and image are deleted 30 days after the discount code is issued

2. Where data is stored

All data the app stores is kept on your Mac, in the following location isolated for the app by the App Sandbox.

~/Library/Containers/com.o2z0.ClipDex.direct/Data/Library/Application Support/com.o2z0.ClipDex.direct/

No sync mechanism, including iCloud, is used. Exported files and backup files are saved wherever you choose.

4. What is not stored by default (opt-in)

The following three are all off initially and only begin storing content once you explicitly turn them on in Settings.

  • Clipboard history — the text you copied, together with its formatting data (RTF / HTML)
  • Image clips — images you copied
  • Paste records — the text you pasted (Pro only)

Unless you turn these on, all the app stores from copying and pasting are numbers — counts, character counts, times of day, content kinds — and app identifiers. The content itself is not stored. The text is read briefly to count characters and determine its kind, then discarded immediately.

About formatting data: While clipboard history is enabled, the app stores not only the text you copied but also the formatting data (RTF / HTML) that the source app placed on the clipboard. Formatting data is stored exactly as the source app produced it; the app neither parses nor modifies it.

As a result, that data may contain information embedded by the source app. None of it is added by ClipDex.

  • The URLs of any links inside the range you copied
  • The path of a local temporary file, which includes your macOS user name (Word and Excel in the developer's own measurements)
  • The name and version of the app used to copy

Stored formatting data is never transmitted, just like every other kind of data (see section 1). There is no separate switch for formatting data; it follows the clipboard history switch. The size limit is 2 MB per format in history and 512 KB per format when an item is moved into Saved Items. Beyond that, only the formatting for that format is dropped and the plain text is stored as usual.

5. How sensitive content is kept out

Even with the opt-in features above turned on, the app excludes sensitive information from its records in the following ways.

  • Built-in app exclusions — anything copied in one of eight apps, mostly password managers (1Password 7, 1Password 8 and later, Bitwarden, KeePassXC, LastPass for Safari, Dashlane, Keychain Access, and System Settings), is not stored, and neither is anything pasted into them
  • Respecting markers set by the source app — when the app you copied from attaches the industry-standard "this is confidential" marker, the content is not stored in clipboard history. However, this only works when the source app supports it, so it cannot detect confidential data in every app
  • Exclusions you add yourself — from Settings you can pick a running app and add it to the exclusion list (in addition to the eight built-in ones)
  • Pause — from the menu bar you can pause recording for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour (there is no indefinite pause)

6. About permissions

The app asks for the macOS Accessibility permission, for these three purposes only.

  • Automatic pasting — sends the ⌘V keystroke to paste the item you picked into the previous app
  • Paste Stack — detects ⌘V pressed in the target app to paste stacked items in order
  • Paste tracking — counts how many times, at what time, and into which app ⌘V is pressed

⌘V is the only keystroke handled. Any other keystroke macOS reports is ignored without being processed, recorded or sent. The app works without the permission: picking an item then copies it and brings the previous app to the front, and you press ⌘V yourself (Paste Stack and paste tracking are unavailable). You can revoke the permission at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

7. Deleting your data

Settings → Data → "All Data" deletes usage statistics, clipboard history (including image clips) and paste records together. Saved Items and your license key are not included. Delete Saved Items from the list, and remove the license key in Settings → General → "ClipDex Pro".

Moving the app to the Trash does not remove the data in the storage location in section 2; macOS keeps it. To remove everything, delete "All Data" and your Saved Items before uninstalling, or delete the ~/Library/Containers/com.o2z0.ClipDex.direct folder in Finder.

To have order records on the license server (section 1-2) deleted, contact us as described in section 9. After deletion, license keys can no longer be resent and renewals can no longer be bought.

8. Migrating from the App Store version

Settings → Data → "Migrate from App Store Version…" copies the App Store version's data on the same Mac (usage statistics, settings, clipboard history, image clips, Saved Items and paste records) into the app's storage location. This happens entirely on your Mac, without network access. The App Store version's data is not deleted. Data the app had before migration is kept for recovery next to the storage location in section 2 (in a folder whose name starts with com.o2z0.ClipDex.direct.pre-migration- inside the same Application Support folder). "All Data" does not delete this folder, so delete it in Finder when you no longer need it.
