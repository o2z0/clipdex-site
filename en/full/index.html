PRIVACY

Your clipboard data never leaves your Mac

History, Saved Items and statistics all stay on your Mac.
No analytics server, no account.

The only network use is update checks

Once a day the app checks for a new version (you can turn this off). Updates install only after you confirm. History and Saved Items are never sent.

What Accessibility is used for

Only three things: sending ⌘V to paste automatically, detecting ⌘V for Paste Stack, and counting pastes. No keystroke other than ⌘V is handled.

Works without Accessibility permission

Picking an item copies it and returns to the previous app, so you just press ⌘V. Paste Stack and paste tracking are unavailable.

Saving content is your choice

You decide whether the actual content you copy or paste is kept.

Password managers are excluded

Eight apps, including 1Password, Bitwarden and Keychain Access, are excluded by default. You can add more.

Pause any time

Pause for 15 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour from the menu bar. Statistics, clipboard history and paste records can each be deleted in one step.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the Full version differ from the App Store version?

The biggest difference is whether picking an item pastes it for you.

  • Full version (this page): picking an item switches back to the previous app and pastes it. Paste Stack (paste in order) and paste tracking and records are included. It uses the macOS Accessibility permission
  • App Store version: picking an item copies it and switches back to the previous app; you press ⌘V yourself. No Paste Stack or paste tracking. In exchange, it asks for no permissions at all

Everything else, including history, Saved Items, search and reports, is the same. The App Store version lacks automatic pasting due to App Store review*1.

*1 In the August 2026 App Store review, the app was rejected under App Store Review Guideline 2.4.5 for using Accessibility features for purposes other than accessibility. The review specifically cited automatic pasting and counting ⌘V. Apple advised that the standard approach for clipboard managers is to place the item on the pasteboard and let the user press ⌘V. Some clipboard managers on the App Store do paste automatically, but ClipDex removed these features from its App Store version to comply with the review, and distributes the full-featured version on this site.

How much rich text formatting is kept when I copy text?

Text with bold, colors or links is kept with its formatting. Pasting keeps formatting by default; hold ⌥ while choosing to paste plain text just that once (you can swap the default in Settings).

Copying with ⌘C always keeps formatting regardless of that setting. Pasting several items together, or a saved item whose text you have edited, gives plain text.

How are images saved?

Images, including JPEGs, are converted to PNG*2 when saved. When you pass an image to another app as a file, it keeps its original name with the extension changed (photo.jpg → photo.png).

Saving images is off by default; turn it on in Settings. Images are counted separately from text clips.

*2 PNG is a lossless format. Copied images arrive in many forms, such as JPEG or TIFF, depending on the app, so ClipDex stores them in one format without losing quality. As a result, photos can take more space than the original JPEG.

Can I move my data from the App Store version?

Yes. Quit the App Store version, then choose Settings → Data → “Migrate from App Store Version…”. The history, Saved Items, settings and report statistics of the App Store version on the same Mac are copied over.

Migration happens entirely on your Mac, without any network access, and the App Store version’s data stays as it is. History entries for copied files (such as files from Finder) cannot keep their link to the original file and are treated as text.

Pro bought in the App Store version does not carry over. If you own App Store Pro, you can buy a license at 50% off.

What’s the difference between Free and Pro?

How much you can keep, for how long, and a few features.

  • Clipboard history (text): 30 items free / 50–1,000 with Pro (200 by default)
  • Clipboard history (images): 15 images, 50 MB total, 10 MB each free / 50–500 images, 1 GB total, 100 MB each with Pro
  • Saved Items (text): 10 free / 300 with Pro
  • Saved Items (images and videos): 5 free / 300 with Pro; up to 100 MB per image and 500 MB per video

Clipboard history drops the oldest items once it reaches the limit. Saved Items are never removed; you simply can’t add more once you reach the limit.
