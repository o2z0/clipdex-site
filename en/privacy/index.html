ClipDex Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

日本語

ClipDex ("the app") manages clipboard history and saved snippets on macOS. By its very nature, the app touches highly sensitive information. That is why it was built from the start around a single principle: none of the data it collects is ever sent anywhere.

1. Nothing is transmitted

The only network activity related to the app is purchasing and restoring a purchase through Apple's App Store, and that communication is handled by Apple's own mechanism (StoreKit) at the system level, not by the app. The developer operates no servers of any kind. The app itself is built without the entitlement to make outgoing network connections, so such connections are not permitted at the operating-system level.

Consequently, none of the data the app collects is sent to the developer or to any third party. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no external crash reporting.

2. Where data is stored

Everything the app stores stays inside your Mac, in a location isolated for this app alone by the App Sandbox:

~/Library/Containers/com.o2z0.ClipDex/Data/Library/Application Support/com.o2z0.ClipDex/

The macOS App Sandbox keeps this area separate from other apps. No synchronization mechanism is used, including iCloud.

4. What is not stored by default (opt-in)

The following two are all off initially and only begin storing content once you explicitly turn them on in Settings.

  • Clipboard history — the text you copied, together with its formatting data (RTF / HTML)
  • Image clips — images you copied

Unless you turn these on, all the app stores are numbers — counts, character counts, times of day — and the identifiers of the apps you copied from. The content itself is not stored.

About formatting data (added in version 1.1): While clipboard history is enabled, the app stores not only the text you copied but also the formatting data (RTF / HTML) that the source app placed on the clipboard. Formatting data is stored exactly as the source app produced it; the app neither parses nor modifies it. As a result, that data may contain the URLs of any links inside the range you copied and, depending on the app (Word and Excel in the developer's own measurements), the path of a local temporary file (which includes your macOS user name) or the name and version of the app used to copy — in other words, information embedded by the source app. None of it is added by ClipDex. Stored formatting data is never transmitted, just like every other kind of data (see section 1). There is no separate switch for formatting data; it follows the clipboard history switch. The size limit is 2 MB per format in history and 512 KB per format when an item is moved into Saved Items. Beyond that, only the formatting for that format is dropped and the plain text is stored as usual.

5. How sensitive content is kept out

Even with the opt-in features above turned on, the app excludes sensitive information from its records in the following ways.

  • Built-in app exclusions — anything copied in one of eight apps, mostly password managers (1Password 7, 1Password 8 and later, Bitwarden, KeePassXC, LastPass for Safari, Dashlane, Keychain Access, and System Settings), is not stored
  • Respecting markers set by the source app — when the app you copied from attaches the industry-standard "this is confidential" marker, the content is not stored. However, this only works when the source app supports it, so it cannot detect confidential data in every app
  • Exclusions you add yourself — from Settings you can pick a running app and add it to the exclusion list (in addition to the eight built-in ones)
  • Pause — from the menu bar you can pause recording for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour (there is no indefinite pause)

6. About permissions

ClipDex does not request any special macOS permissions (such as Accessibility), and it never monitors keyboard input. Pasting an item you selected from history or Saved Items works by copying the item and bringing the previous app to the front; you press ⌘V yourself (an on-screen confirmation explains this each time).

7. Deleting your data

From "All Data" in Settings you can delete usage statistics and clipboard history (including image clips) together. Saved Items are not covered by that action; delete them individually from the list instead. Uninstalling the app removes the storage location described above along with it. Nothing remains with the developer — because nothing was ever sent.
